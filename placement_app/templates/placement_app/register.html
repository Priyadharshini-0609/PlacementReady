Placement Preparation & Readiness Platform

A personalized platform designed to help college students prepare for campus placements through customized preparation roadmaps, mock tests, interview practice, and real-time placement readiness analytics!

Key Features:
Diagnostic Skill Assessment Evaluate the student's current aptitude, technical, coding, communication, and reasoning skills.
Target Role-Based Preparation Roadmaps Generate a structured Day 1 to Day N preparation plan based on the student's selected career role.
Smart Weak-Skill Recommendations Identify skill gaps and recommend specific topics and activities for improvement.
Mock Tests & Assessments Practice aptitude, technical, coding, and role-specific tests.
Interview Round Practice Prepare for HR, technical, and managerial interview rounds.
Real-Time Readiness Analytics Track preparation progress, test performance, skill improvement, and overall placement readiness.
